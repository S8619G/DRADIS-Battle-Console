# Combat Tuning Test Changelog (2026-10-02)

Builds on the Firewall & Combat Test.

## Changed

- DRADIS title centered over the TACTICAL DEFENSE CONSOLE line.
- FTL readout moved right (0.135 to 0.18 of the outline width) to center it in the stern section.
- Defense Battery line replaced by a smooth arc over the port side of the ship, at least 15 pixels from the hull and reaching slightly past the stern and bow.
- The arc flickers in yellow and orange shades with brief bright sparkles while the battery fires; its width stays fixed.
- Raptors: 7 hit points (was 4); they keep 75% evasion.
- Vipers: 2 hit points (was 4), with a new 20% chance to dodge Raider fire.
- Raider hit chance against Vipers and Raptors raised from 35% to 50% (before dodging).

## Added

- Inspector settings for the flak arc: Defense Arc Projection, Overhang and Width, Flak Flicker Hz, Flak Sparkle Amount and the four fire colors.
- Viper Evasion Chance setting.
