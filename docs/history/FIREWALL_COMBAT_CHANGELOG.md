# Firewall & Combat Test Changelog (2026-10-02)

Builds on the Waves & High Scores Test.

## Added

- FIREWALL button (between DEFENSE BATTERY and LAUNCH VIPERS). Usable once a Heavy Raider begins hacking; slows the hack and the hull drain to a quarter speed. Uses up in 4 seconds, recharges in 8 seconds (faster than the Defense Battery), and can be reused from 25%. Plays an original electronic defense sound while on.
- INTRUSION DETECTION box in the center of the ship outline while a Heavy Raider hacks or drains: red outline, thick 45-degree red and black stripes, flashing text.
- Fighter losses: Vipers and Raptors have 4 hit points and can be shot down by Raider attacks (35% hit chance; Raptors keep their 75% evasion) and, for Raptors, Basestar flak. Each loss costs 200 points (Viper) or 400 points (Raptor); the score never drops below zero and bonus-repair progress is kept. Damaged fighters show their remaining strength in their label.
- RAPTORS LOST counter in the left sidebar alongside VIPERS LOST.

## Changed

- Title reads DRADIS instead of BSG // DRADIS.
- SHIP STATUS text beside the ship outline removed.
- FTL and HULL readouts centered in the left and right sections of the ship outline (adjustable).
- Resurrection Ship now crosses the scope horizontally from a random side in about a minute, firing as it goes, and leaves without points when it reaches the other side.
- Resurrection Ship barrages: 3 missiles fired one per second, every 8 seconds (shortened by the wave, always faster than Basestars), fanned out instead of in a line, 5 hull damage each (Basestar missiles do 10).
- Resurrection Ship frequency rises with the wave: arrival chance 25% in wave 2 rising to 60%; quiet period 90 seconds falling to 40 seconds.
- Resurrection Ship hit points 16 to 10, so a focused attack can destroy it before it escapes.
- Raptors take 4 unevaded hits instead of 2.
- Six bottom buttons share the console width.
